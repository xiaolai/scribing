# Data notices

The engine's MIT LICENSE does not replace data-source terms. Every optional pack has a full adjacent `generated/<pack-id>.NOTICE.txt`; every pinned input has `sources/<source-id>.LICENSE`. Their hashes are checked by `scripts/data/check.mjs`.

Sources:
- Robin Linacre, letterpaths — https://github.com/RobinL/letterpaths — MIT.
- Anas, glyphed.js — https://github.com/a-elhaag/glyphed.js — ISC.
- Ulrich Apel and KanjiVG contributors — https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/ and https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg — CC-BY-SA-3.0. Scribing's optional packs transform ordered SVG centerlines into sampled polylines. Attribution and ShareAlike apply to these transformed data.
- Brenden Lake, Omniglot — https://github.com/brendenlake/omniglot — MIT. Research reference: Lake, Salakhutdinov and Tenenbaum (2015), Human-level concept learning through probabilistic program induction, Science 350(6266), 1332–1338.

The manifests disclose transformations and selected source paths. All data are marked technical previews. No source is described as a native-reviewed curriculum.
